# Support and community

## Join the conversation

- **[Official Discord](https://discord.gg/9VyUNRk7N)** — quick conversation, shared discoveries and community presence.
- **[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/marquezinii/allhailthecone/discussions)** — durable questions, lore interpretations and proposals that benefit from an archive.
- **[Issue tracker](https://github.com/marquezinii/allhailthecone/issues)** — reproducible site defects, accessibility barriers and documentation corrections.

Before posting, read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Do not use community channels for security reports; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Asking a useful question

Include the relevant page, record, character or canon document; explain what you observed; and say whether you need a factual correction, an interpretation or a creative discussion. The Cone provides direction, not complete answers.
