# Offices, orders and agreements

## The Crown & Kettle

**Canon.** Aurelian's convening office. It coordinates diplomacy, hears petitions and brings competing responsibilities to the same table. Its emblem pairs a crown with a cup; it does not claim to own the Cone. Authority is exercised through public commitments rather than divine certainty.

## Bread Operations

**Canon.** Led by COO Percival Crumb. Its responsibility is provisioning: bread, reserves, routes, deliveries and the people who make them possible. The department's language is corporate because reliable food requires coordination. Its measures should remain connected to actual people fed.

Its recurring tension is between efficient central plans and the knowledge of local bakers, ferrymen and residents.

## The Potassium Division

**Canon.** K. Musa's infrastructure office. Maintains systems, schedules repairs and publishes service windows. It has a duty to explain disruptions in terms travellers can use. A perfectly functioning machine serving the wrong need is still a failed service.

## The Little Order

**Canon.** An independent devotional and maintenance community. It tends the shrine and welcomes visitors of any belief. Joining a ceremony is voluntary. The Order may advise the crown, but it cannot turn hospitality into a test of faith.

There is no established supreme robot, enforced creed or hidden military command.

## The Office of Resonance

**Canon.** Octavia's network of musicians, ceremonial hosts and cultural messengers. Makes public celebrations possible and helps communities preserve distinct traditions. Culture is not merely an instrument of the crown.

## The Office of Continuity

**Canon.** Keepers of records, revisions and unresolved questions. They distinguish witnessed facts from plausible reports without pretending to omniscience. They are responsible for labelling uncertainty, not abolishing it.

The website's archive styling borrows their visual language. Real project maintenance remains separate from fictional authority.

## The Lantern Survey

**Semi-Canon.** A loose fellowship of route surveyors, including occasional work by Sir Vesper. They place beacons, test return passages and record what the map cannot yet promise. A map is a commitment to a traveller; an untested line is not a route.
