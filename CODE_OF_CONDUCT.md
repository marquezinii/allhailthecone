# The hospitality compact

Treat participants with respect. Disagreement is welcome; harassment, hate, threats, impersonation, spam and publishing private information are not. Do not submit stolen work or misrepresent authorship. Keep critique specific to the work.

Maintainers may label, move, close or remove material that violates these standards and may restrict repeat offenders. Use GitHub's report and block controls for abuse. Do not repeat sensitive material in a public issue. A private contact channel may be added later; no unmonitored mailbox is advertised here.

Participation in this creative community does not require a belief, purchase or disclosure of personal identity. The Keepers' fictional devotion is not an excuse for real-world coercion.
